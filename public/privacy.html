Privacy

Last updated 26 November 2026.

The short version

We keep what you give us on the waitlist form, we use it to write to you about EYVA, and we don't sell it or share it with anyone else. Reply to any email and we stop.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist: your name, your email address, the colour you'd choose, and, if you add one, the first name of a friend who might want to hear about EYVA. We don't contact the friend; that name is only so we can ask you to pass the word when the time comes. When you fill in the salon form: your name, your salon and its area, and how to reach you.

What we use it for

To tell you when EYVA goes on sale, to hold the launch price for you, and to send you the short notes Val writes along the way. That's all.

Where it lives

Your entry is stored by EYVA Concepts FZ-LLC on Cloudflare's servers, and a copy arrives in our mailbox at val@eyva.me. When the shop opens, the waitlist moves to our shop platform so your launch price can be applied to your order. Nobody else has access.

Cookies

This site sets no tracking cookies and runs no advertising pixels. If that changes, this page will say so before it does.

Your choices

Write to val@eyva.me at any time to see what we hold about you, change it, or have it deleted. To stop hearing from us, reply to any email with a word.

Law

We handle personal data under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021).
